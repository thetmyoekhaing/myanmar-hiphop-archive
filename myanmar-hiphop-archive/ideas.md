# Design direction

## Theme Name
Archive After Dark / Signal in the Static

## Intro
A research publication that feels like a scene map assembled from flyers, tape sleeves, club photos, captions and source receipts—not a polished startup landing page.

## Core principles
- Treat the archive as living, partial and contested.
- Make the five historical periods the primary navigation.
- Use images as evidence and atmosphere, always with source/credit visible.
- Preserve aliases, uncertain dates and source quality instead of silently cleaning them away.
- Make the interface feel closer to a gig wall, zine index and record sleeve than a dashboard.

## Visual system
Black ink, dark rust, nicotine cream, faded blue and mustard yellow. Heavy condensed typography. Thin rules, photocopy-like image treatments, sticker shapes, irregular offsets and high-contrast blocks.

## Layout
A dark editorial shell with a loud typographic hero, overlapping image collage, fact strip, period navigator, image gallery, name wall and expandable receipt ledger.

## Interaction
The period navigator updates the featured research card. Search filters the period cards and name wall. Images open in a lightbox. Source ledger expands in place. Every interactive control remains visibly connected to archive evidence.

## Brand voice
Direct, specific, and transparent: “working archive”, “source says”, “unverified”, “signal”, “receipt”. Avoid “platform”, “solution”, “unlock” and other startup language.
